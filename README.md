## about

A demake of Geometry Dash for the NES using the MMC3 mapper.

## nightly builds

> [!WARNING]
> These are constantly changing and *might* be broken. \
> Your save files are not guaranteed to carry over from one nightly to the next.

### huge ROMs

These ROMs contain all of the levels and music made for Famidash.

Want EVERYTHING? Get the [Huge Man](https://nightly.link/tfdsoft/famidash/workflows/main/main/famidash.huge.nes.zip) for maximum satisfaction. \
Do you have WAY, WAY too many quarters? Play the [Huge Man VS Arcade Version](https://nightly.link/tfdsoft/famidash/workflows/main/main/famidash.vs-huge.nes.zip).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Huge ROMs may not work on some emulators, as well as some flashcarts. Try an accurate emulator like [Mesen](https://www.mesen.ca/) for these huge ROMs. Otherwise, see the individual releases down below.

### individual releases

If you're just looking to play Geometry Dash on your NES and that's it,\
we recommend starting with [The Original Game](https://nightly.link/tfdsoft/famidash/workflows/main/main/famidash.main.nes.zip).

Want more levels? Check out
[B-Sides](https://nightly.link/tfdsoft/famidash/workflows/main/main/famidash.b-sides.nes.zip), 
[C-Sides](https://nightly.link/tfdsoft/famidash/workflows/main/main/famidash.c-sides.nes.zip), 
[D-Sides](https://nightly.link/tfdsoft/famidash/workflows/main/main/famidash.d-sides.nes.zip), and
[E-Sides](https://nightly.link/tfdsoft/famidash/workflows/main/main/famidash.e-sides.nes.zip).

Do you have some quarters left over? Play the [VS Arcade Version](https://nightly.link/tfdsoft/famidash/workflows/main/main/famidash.vs-sys.nes.zip) (with levels from The Original Game).

Want just the music? Download [The Album](https://nightly.link/tfdsoft/famidash/workflows/main/main/famidash.album.nes.zip)!
